## Notes

Roadmap item S-01 of milestone M-01. A learner loads a course and sees its lessons in course order. An open site loads from its URL through a crawl in the worker. A site that blocks automated reads loads through a browser extension that saves the pages the learner opens. Each lesson is stored as Markdown for the later slices.
